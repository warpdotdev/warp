Rail triage — mock

Ranked band

Projects you ranked in the Project Priorities panel sit on top, in your order, with a small rank number. Everything else keeps today's first-seen order below the unranked divider. Nothing ever reorders on agent events — color does the signaling.

Colors

Orange — agent waiting on you (permission prompt / question), with wait age.

Red — still waiting after 5 minutes. Oldest fire reads at a glance.

Green — finished / waiting for the next task, results not yet seen. Clears when you focus the pane.

Blue — working; needs nothing.

The project header inherits its most urgent child (red > orange > green > running), with the wait age of the worst one.

Header chips

⏳ 2 agents blocked anywhere — including unranked projects. Click jumps to the next blocked task, cycling. ✅ 1 the same for done-and-unseen. Navigation affordances, not lists — no duplicate rows.

Not shown (behavior)

  • Urgent sound + OS notification on block: immediate for ranked, 60 s debounce for unranked.
  • Re-notifies every 3 min (ranked) / 15 min (unranked) until you answer the agent. Focusing the pane silences the cycle; wandering off re-arms it.
  • Soft chime once on done. Coalesced banners ("3 agents waiting…"), never one per agent.
  • Sound still plays when Warp is focused but the blocked pane's tab isn't active.
